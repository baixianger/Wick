Codex OAuth integration

The native Swift implementation follows the authentication, credential-store,
usage parsing and provider design of dsh-codex-adapter, and the OAuth/Responses
wire contract implemented by @earendil-works/pi-ai 0.85.1 (MIT, Mario Zechner).
Wick does not embed Node.js, pi-ai, the Codex CLI, or app-server.

Reference projects:
https://github.com/baixianger/dsh-codex-adapter
https://github.com/earendil-works/pi
